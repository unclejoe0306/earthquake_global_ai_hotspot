關於系統About the system

由2026年台灣學者周裕清醫師開發、全球首創的 Web 即時地震監測與碎形訊號前兆分析系統。本系統結合非線性動力學、統計物理學與 3D 視覺化技術，介接 USGS 即時數據；突破傳統單一斷層限制，首創全球強震帶15地理區，以 AI 構建7日驗證窗和中尺度窗，深度解析應力異常，掌握大震前兆。研究指標不等同確定地震預測，實際防災資訊請以官方發布為準。Developed in 2026 by Taiwanese physician and scholar Dr. Yu-Ching Chou, this system is presented as the world's first web platform for real-time earthquake monitoring and fractal precursor analysis. It combines nonlinear dynamics, statistical physics, and 3D visualization with live USGS data. Beyond single-fault analysis, it introduces 15 geographic regions across global strong-earthquake belts and uses AI to construct 7-day validation and mesoscale windows to study stress anomalies and potential precursors. These research indicators do not predict earthquakes with certainty. For practical disaster preparedness, rely on official guidance.
